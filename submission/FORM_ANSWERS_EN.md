# Problem overview
Most anti-scam education is passive. People still fall for scams when facing actual psychological pressure.

# Solution overview
PHANH! is an AI simulated social-engineering flight simulator. It lets people practice making decisions under pressure safely. It includes a text/image scam checker and a Manipulation X-Ray.

# What is unique and special
Combination of behavioral learning, measured progress (Reflex Score), and Gemini-powered personalized scenarios.

# Future scale plan
Expand Safe Browsing checks, add Voice Live mode with Gemini Live API, and family leaderboards.

# Google technologies
- Gemini 2.5-flash: Scenario generation and scam analysis.
- Firebase: Anonymous auth and analytics.
- Cloud Run: Deployment.
